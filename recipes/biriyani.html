BIRIYANI RECIPE

HOME PAGE

DESCRIPTION

Chicken biriyani is the household dish in India and especially in my side. We celebrate stuff by making biriyani, few people can survive their whole life with just biriyani. LET US SEE HOW DO WE MAKE IT BELOW

INGREDIENTS

  • Chicken - 500 grams
  • Basmathi Rice - 1kg
  • oninion , tamato , coriander, other veggies
  • Masala items

STEPS

  1. Wash everything out there and cut veggies
  2. Add masala to a cooker then add chicken
  3. Make a fine paste and add it to cooker with water
  4. Now add Rice and cook upto 3-4 whistels
    5. VOIILA !! YOUR BIRIYANI IS READYYY
