HOME PAGE

Description

A Simple easy to cook recipie , with most minimal ingredients and efforts PRO TIP : Make it spicy and eat it hot. Lets see how do we do it

INGREDIENTS

  • A Maggi packet
  • Spices
  • Soy sauce , Red chilli Sauce , Tamato Sauce
  • Sesame seeds and oregano

STEPS

  1. Boil your Maggi and soak it Dry
  2. Mix all the spices and sauces
  3. Add hot oil to the mixture , then mix it with Maggi
