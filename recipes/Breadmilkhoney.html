Description

My Favourite and most easy recipe . even a dumb person. can make this recipe. It requires very minimal effort and Time and ingredients and yet give the best taste/output. Lets now See The Quick Recipe for MILK TOAST

INGREDIENTS

  • MILK -500 ml
  • Bread - 4-6 pcs
  • Honey - 3 tabelspoon

STEPS

  1. Roast the bread on pan for few minutes with ghee
  2. Pour the milk on bread whick is on the pan and let it soak in it
  3. Take out the bread on a plate when it turns golden and add honey for extra sweetness
